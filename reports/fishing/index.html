What the Weekly Fishing Report covers

Every Wednesday, free, and it runs the whole state rather than one lake or one stretch of coast. Central Valley reservoirs, the Sierra and Eastern Sierra, the Delta, and the coast from Fort Bragg down to San Diego and the Coronado Islands. Each issue carries water temperature and level, what is biting and where, the depths and presentations that are producing, and season and regulation changes as they land.
