How to read this map. Pressure levels are a B Mello editorial ranking built from county sheriff and district attorney ag-crime unit reporting, task force case activity, and press releases published through early August 2026. Marker placement is the county centroid; markers are not to county scale. Reported figures understate actual loss — a large share of ag crime is never reported. Verify any specific case with the responsible agency.
