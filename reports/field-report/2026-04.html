Section 1

Crop Status & Growing Season

April is one of the highest-leverage months in the Central Valley — nearly every commodity is in a critical growth phase simultaneously. Temperatures warming into the 70–80°F range, soil moisture from winter rains, and long daylight hours combine to drive explosive biomass accumulation. Watch irrigation timing closely as evapotranspiration climbs rapidly.

🍐  Almonds Nut Set
Petal fall complete. Jackets dropping. Hull and shell development in full swing — most water-sensitive period of the season. Confirm adequate pollination; identify small nut counts now. Watch for navel orangeworm egg lay and mite monitoring. Confirm irrigation scheduling for hull split push starting in May.
🌿  Pistachios Bloom / Pollination
Peak bloom mid-April. Wind-driven pollen transfer occurring now — avoid orchard disturbance. 2026 is an “on-year” in the alternate-bearing cycle; yield potential elevated. Monitor for Botryosphaeria panicle & shoot blight. Minimal irrigation during bloom to avoid soil cracking near root zone.
🪓  Walnuts Catkin / Bud Swell
Chandler and Howard breaking bud. Catkin elongation underway — critical window for codling moth first flight. Deploy degree-day traps now. Watch for walnut blight (Xanthomonas) if late rains hit. Verify chill hour accumulation; any deficit means uneven leafout and reduced fruit set.
🍇  Wine & Table Grapes Bud Break
Bud break to 2–4 inch shoot growth. Frost risk persists through mid-April — monitor overnight temps closely. Most frost-vulnerable period. Begin powdery mildew spray program at 0.5-inch shoot. Coachella table grape harvest ramping; SJV vineyards in early growth stage.
🥝  Kiwis Shoot Growth / Pre-Bloom
Rapid vegetative shoot growth phase. Training and tying new canes is a priority this month. Begin thinning overcrowded fruiting canes. Pollinator bee hive placement should be scheduled — bloom is 3–5 weeks out. Botrytis pressure rises with April fog events; monitor canopy microclimate.
🍊  Citrus Bloom / Petal Fall
Orange and navel bloom peak in April. Regulate irrigation to mild stress leading into bloom, then ease off at full petal drop. Monitor for citrus thrips during fruit set. Asian Citrus Psyllid (HLB vector) monitoring is a top priority — report suspicious symptoms to CDFA immediately.
🌿  Cotton Pre-Plant / Planting
Soil temps need to hit 65°F at 4-inch depth — typically achieved mid-to-late April in Kern and Kings counties. Final pre-plant herbicide applications due. Soil prep and bed shaping should be complete. Planting window opens last 10 days of April. Wait for stable soil temps.
🌾  Wheat (Winter) Jointing / Heading
Stem elongation to early head emergence. Apply final top-dress N if not done in March. Scout heavily for stripe rust and powdery mildew — fungicide window is tight at flag leaf through heading. Aphid populations build fast in warm April weather. Check soil moisture at depth.

Section 2

April Fertilizer Requirements

April is peak demand season for most crops. Nutrient uptake curves are steep — timing and placement are critical. Fertigate where possible to match uptake to demand. Soil and tissue testing from March should be guiding your rates.

Crop Primary Nutrients Micros / Notes Timing & Method
Almonds N: 40–60 lbs/ac split via fertigation through July. K critical post–petal fall for hull sizing. Zn & B foliar at petal fall HIGH. Ca foliar for shell integrity. Split fertigation every 10–14 days via drip. First K push now through May.
Pistachios Minimal during bloom. Post-bloom K & Ca become priority. No foliar during bloom. Zn correction if leaf tissue deficient. HIGH Hold N & heavy fertigation during bloom. Resume aggressively post-petal fall.
Walnuts N: 80–100 lbs/ac season. Deliver 30% in April via fertigation. K demand increases with catkin extension. Zn foliar at catkin stage. Mn if deficient. MED Fertigation via micro-sprinklers or drip. First 30% N dose applied now.
Grapes N: 30–50 lbs/ac; apply 40% in April as shoots push. K demand begins at 4-inch shoot. B foliar at pre-bloom critical for fruit set. HIGH Zn & Mg via foliar if deficient. Fertigation and/or dry broadcast. Foliar micro sprays before bloom, not during.
Kiwis High N demand during shoot growth — 40 lbs/ac N in April–May. K essential for vine structure. B & Ca foliar ahead of bloom. HIGH Mg via fertigation if pale leaf color noted. Split fertigations every 2 weeks. Foliar nutrients 2–3 weeks before first bloom.
Citrus N: moderate — 20–25 lbs/ac in April. Avoid excess N at bloom. Ca & Mg important. Zn & Mn foliar post-petal fall. MED Iron chelate if chlorosis in young leaves. Fertigation. No heavy N during peak bloom; resume at petal fall.
Cotton Pre-plant: P & K incorporated if soil test warrants. At emergence: starter N (15–20 lbs/ac). Zn pre-plant or in-furrow. MED B pre-plant on deficient soils. Pre-plant broadcast & incorporate. In-furrow starter at planting.
Wheat Final top-dress N: 30–40 lbs/ac UAN at flag leaf. HIGH S: 10–15 lbs/ac if not in base program. Foliar Mn & Zn if flag leaf tissue shows deficiency. Mn particularly important on alkaline soils. Ground or aerial at flag leaf (Zadoks GS 37–39). UAN 28% via streamer bars.
Advisor Note: April soil temperatures and tissue tests from late March should drive final rate decisions. Water stress during fertigation events reduces uptake efficiency by 30–50%. Always confirm irrigation scheduling before injecting — nutrients sitting dry in soil don’t benefit your crop.
