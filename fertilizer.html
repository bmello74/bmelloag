Fertilizer & Soil Amendments

Plant nutrition and soil health — the material, priced and delivered.

The center of our business is plant nutrition and soil health. We sell the dry fertilizers and soil amendments that feed a crop and correct the ground it grows in — and across the Central Valley we put them out ourselves.

What we sell

  • Gypsum
  • Limestone
  • Sulfur
  • Compost
  • Dry fertilizers and blends
  • Biological and humic products

If you are pricing a program, call us. We source material and quote it against what your ground actually needs.

Where we sell

Fertilizer and amendment sales cover all of California and into the western states. Custom application runs through the Central Valley, Bakersfield to Madera — tractor spreaders in trees, vines and kiwis, spreader trucks on open ground.

Start with the soil, not the invoice

The cheapest ton of material is the one you did not need. Soil and water testing shows what the ground gave up last season and what it is short of now — the difference between a program and a guess. We can arrange sampling and go through the results with you.

The same thinking runs through our Field Report every month: crop stage, what should be going on the ground, and what materials are doing in the market.
