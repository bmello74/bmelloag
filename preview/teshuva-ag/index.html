Four reasons to go Teshuva

Made on the farm

FPE+ is fermented on site in Hanford from plant material grown on naturally, regeneratively farmed ground. We use it on our own crops first.

Feed the soil, not just the plant

Our approach comes from Korean Natural Farming: build a living soil and the crop grows strong enough to handle pests, disease and tough weather.

Physics in the water

As a dealer for Pursanova and PhysaGro, we use activated water technology that restores water without chemicals, magnets or electronics.

Straight answers
