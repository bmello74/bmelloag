Faith, physics and living soil

Teshuva Ag is run by Eddie Esajian of Esajian Family Farms in Hanford, California.

Why Teshuva

Teshuva is a Hebrew word for return. For us it means returning soil and water to the way they were designed to work: alive, balanced and able to carry a crop without a long list of chemical fixes.

We redefine what is possible for your soil and water. As a dealer for Pursanova and PhysaGro, we use physics to turn environmental challenges into fertile ground. Our inputs naturally improve growing conditions and nutrient uptake so plants grow in the healthiest, most sustainable way.

Tested on our own ground

FPE+ is produced on the farm in Hanford. The alfalfa field we harvest to make FPE+ Alfalfa gets an application of the finished product after every cutting, so the product improves as the crop improves.

We have run FPE+ bloom sprays on almonds, cherries, peaches, blueberries, apples, quince and jujubes. What we sell is what we use.

Rooted in Korean Natural Farming

Our FPE+ line is inspired by the Korean Natural Farming methods developed by Master Han-Kyu Cho. KNF builds soil health with natural inputs: indigenous microorganisms, fermented plant and fruit juices, and lactic acid bacteria. Feed the soil, and the plant grows strong enough to resist pests, disease and mold on its own.
