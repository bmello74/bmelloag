FPE+ fermented plant extracts

Living soil probiotics and biostimulants for commercial growers.

Download grower guide (PDF) Request a quote

FPE+ is our enhanced line of fermented plant extracts. We lacto-ferment fresh plant material from naturally, regeneratively farmed crops using Pursanova activated water. Fermentation breaks plant compounds down into forms soil biology and crops can use right away: polyphenols, organic acids, amino acids, enzymes and beneficial microbes.

  • Builds soil biology. More diversity and larger populations of beneficial microbes in the root zone.
  • Roots and quality. Supports root development, nutrient density, and fragrance, flavor and color in the crop.
  • Bloom spray. We have used FPE+ as a bloom spray in almonds, cherries, peaches, blueberries, apples, quince and jujubes, with crop protection results comparable to conventional fungicide programs.
  • Compost. Speeds the breakdown of organic matter and compost piles.

CDFA-registered organic input

Biochar

Hold water. Build carbon. Support soil biology.

Download sell sheet (PDF) Request a quote

Forest-biomass wood biochar, screened to 1/4 inch minus. A porous, stable carbon that stays in your soil and holds water and nutrients in the root zone. Apply it alone, biologically charged with FPE+ right before spreading, or blended with your compost or manure for one pass.

  • Water under SGMA. Published research shows plant-available water gains of about 45% in sandy soils, 21% in medium soils and 14% in fine soils.
  • Long-lived carbon. H:Corg of 0.20, well under the 0.4 threshold for carbon that lasts beyond 100 years.
  • Clean and low salt. ECe of 0.22 and pH 8.13. No added salt load.
  • Cost share. Fits NRCS EQIP Practice 336 and the CDFA Healthy Soils biochar practice. We help with the planning and paperwork.

Biochar is a soil-building investment, not a yield booster in a bag. It works best in coarse and medium soils, and we will tell you straight whether your ground is a good fit. Delivered to Hanford; growers outside Hanford, call for delivered pricing.

Nitrogen stabilizer

RSTC 17

Pay for it with the nitrogen you are already buying.

Download almond trial (PDF) Request a quote

RSTC 17 goes in with your UAN-32 at 1 oz per gallon. In a 2024 almond trial in Newman, CA (Independence on Hanson-536), plots that cut UAN-32 by 25% and added RSTC 17 were statistically equal in yield to full-rate nitrogen plots.

TreatmentKernel yield, lb/acre
75% N3,093
100% N3,222
75% N + RSTC 173,265
100% N + RSTC 173,516

The 25% of UAN-32 you do not buy covers the cost of RSTC 17, so it adds no new line to the fertilizer budget.

One orchard, one season. Your results will vary with soil, water, variety and management. Trial by Bisabri Ag Research and Consulting. RSTC 17 is a CarbonWorks product.
