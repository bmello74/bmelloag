From gypsum supplier to plant health partner

Superior Soil Supplements started out selling dry soil amendments, mostly gypsum and compost, to growers in the Central Valley. Load by load it grew into one of the major gypsum suppliers in California. Today the focus is the whole plant: nutrition and plant health programs built from each grower’s own soil and water.

How we got here

The company was built on a simple idea: get growers good material, on time, at a fair delivered price. Gypsum was the product Valley ground needed most, so gypsum became the core of the business.

To keep up with demand, Superior Soil went to the source. Empire gypsum is mined in Empire, Nevada and shipped into California on 100-car unit trains. Two strategically placed rail terminals, in Mendota and Wasco, unload those trains so gypsum can be trucked to farms from the west side to the south Valley.

Around that backbone the company added wood chips, bark and commercial landscape products, fertilizer, solution grade products and the silos, tanks and equipment to run them, along with the service to keep that equipment working.

The newest and most promising part of the company is the agronomy department, and it now sets the direction for everything else. GPS-guided soil sampling, water and saturated paste testing, and custom blends written by an in-house agronomist and Certified Crop Adviser let us feed exactly what a crop needs for yield and plant health, and skip what it does not.

What has not changed

  • Hanford headquarters, Valley people
  • Conventional and organic growers served side by side
  • Delivered pricing you can plan around
  • Products backed by guaranteed analysis and lab data
