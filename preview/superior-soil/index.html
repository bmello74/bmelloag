A program built on your numbers

Our agronomy department starts with your soil and water, not a product list. Every recommendation ties back to a test result and a GPS point you can return to next season.

  1. Sample

    We pull soil from georeferenced points so the same spots are tested every year.

  2. Test

    Standard soil, irrigation water and saturated paste analyses.

  3. Read

    Our CCA reviews the results against your crop, yield goal and water.

  4. Blend

    A custom dry or solution blend that supplies what is short and nothing that is not.

  5. Feed and re-test

    Delivered, spread or run through your water, then re-tested to track progress.
