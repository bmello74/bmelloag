Limestone and dolomite

Raise pH on acid ground and correct the calcium to magnesium balance. Dolomite brings magnesium along with calcium; limestone does not.

Sulfur

Elemental sulfur oxidizes to lower pH and release iron, zinc and manganese tied up in alkaline soils. Sulfate sulfur, as in gypsum, feeds the plant without acidifying.

Compost and manure

Green waste, dairy and steer compost plus duck manure build organic matter and biology. Superior All-Green Compost is a CDFA registered organic input material.

KMI Zeolite

Natural clinoptilolite from Nevada that holds ammonium, potassium and water in the root zone and releases them as the crop needs them.

Biochar

Forest-biomass biochar screened to a quarter inch. It adds lasting pore space for water and nutrients and blends well with compost and manure.

Biologicals

ZelTec, Nitryx SP, MegaPhos SP, Armory SP and fermented plant extracts to rebuild soil biology and nutrient cycling.
