How a program comes together

  1. GPS sampling

    We map your blocks and pull cores from georeferenced points, so next year’s samples come from the same spots and the data is comparable.

  2. Lab analysis

    Soil, irrigation water and saturated paste tests run on the panel your crop and concerns call for.

  3. CCA review

    Our Certified Crop Adviser reads soil and water together against your crop, yield goal and history.

  4. Program and blend

    You get a written fertility program and a custom blend priced delivered: dry, solution, or both.

  5. Re-test

    Season over season, the same points show what changed and where to adjust.

What each test tells you

Most problems in Valley ground come from the soil and the water acting on each other. Testing both is how you find the real cause.

Standard soil analysis

Fertility and chemistry in the root zone.

  • pH and organic matter
  • Estimated nitrogen release
  • Cation exchange capacity and base saturation
  • Available nitrogen (NO3-N and NH4-N)
  • Mehlich III P, K, Ca, Mg, Na, S
  • Zn, Mn, Fe, Cu, B, Al, Mo, Co, Se, Cl

Irrigation water analysis

What your water adds to, or takes from, your soil every set.

  • pH, pHc, hardness and conductivity
  • SAR, adjusted SAR and RSC
  • Calcium, magnesium, potassium, sodium
  • Carbonate, bicarbonate, chloride, sulfate
  • Boron, nitrate-N, phosphorus
  • Iron, manganese, copper, zinc, aluminum

Saturated paste test

The salinity picture the way the roots see it.

  • Soil salinity (ECe)
  • Sodium hazard and SAR of the soil solution
  • Soluble calcium, magnesium and sodium
  • Chloride and boron toxicity risk
  • Gypsum and leaching requirement
