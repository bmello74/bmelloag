To haul for Superior Soil, email dispatch@superiorsoil.com with your carrier information and these three documents:

  • W-9
  • Operating authority
  • Certificate of insurance

Carrier terms are net 30 from receipt of invoice. Trailers must arrive clean; drivers are responsible for any contamination of the load.
