Sampling instructions

Soil

  • Take 15 to 20 cores from one uniform area for each sample. Split fields with different soils or history.
  • Sample 0 to 12 inches unless your agronomist asks for other depths. In orchards and vines, sample inside the wetted zone.
  • Stay away from fertilizer bands, field edges, roads and wet spots.
  • Mix the cores in a clean plastic bucket and fill a zip bag with about 2 cups. Use about 1 quart if you want a saturated paste test.

Water

  • Run the pump at least 15 minutes before sampling.
  • Rinse a clean plastic bottle three times with the water, then fill it to the top (at least 16 oz) and cap it.
  • Keep it cold and get it to us within 48 hours.

Shipping

  • Label every bag and bottle with the sample ID from your sheet.
  • Pack water bottles with an ice pack and put the printed sheet in the box.
  • Ship early in the week so samples do not sit over a weekend.
