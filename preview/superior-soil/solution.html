Solution grade products

Finely ground and highly soluble so they go into solution fast and stay out of your filters and emitters.

Soluble biologicals such as ZelTec, Nitryx SP, MegaPhos SP and Armory SP also run through the water. See biologicals.

Why water-run gypsum

Very clean water, like canal and snowmelt supplies, is low in salts and can seal the soil surface so it will not soak in. Sodic or high-bicarbonate well water pushes sodium onto the clay. Small, steady doses of solution gypsum fix both problems by adding calcium to every set.

  • Improves penetration of low-salt water
  • Supplies calcium to replace and leach sodium
  • Spoon-feeds calcium and sulfur during fruit set and sizing
  • No extra trips across the field

Running it well

Manufacturer guidance for solution gypsum, and what our team checks on every install:

  1. Have the water analyzed before setting a rate.
  2. Adjust pH first. For drip and micro systems hold the water around 6.5 to 7.0 before adding any calcium product.
  3. When injecting phosphorus, use an acid form of phosphorus fertilizer.
  4. Use a scale preventative where your water calls for it.
  5. Jar test any new tank mix for compatibility before it goes in the line.
