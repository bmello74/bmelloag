Websites & Marketing

Websites, Google, social media and sales materials for local businesses, from a Hanford company that builds and runs its own.

We built this website ourselves. We also built melloacres.com, we publish a set of email reports every month to Central Valley readers, and we turn vendor proposals into recommendations a board can approve. Now we do the same work for other businesses.

You do not have to be in agriculture. If you run a local business and want more people to find you, see your work and pick up the phone, we can help.

See your website before you decide

Most web designers ask you to sign first and picture it later. We do it the other way around. We build a working draft of your site, with your own photos, your logo and your words, and send you a private link. You open it on your phone, tap through it the way a customer would, and tell us what to change. You decide after you have seen it work.

Draft links are private and kept out of Google until you approve them.

What we offer

Websites

Fast, phone-friendly sites built from your real photos and your real work. Your number and email are one tap away on every page, and a request form sends leads straight to you.

Google and search

Your Google Business Profile set up and verified so you show up on Maps, and pages written the way people search: your service plus your town.

Social media

Facebook and Instagram pages set up to match your site. Post templates so you can run it yourself, or we post for you and run targeted local ads.

Email newsletters

Mailchimp set up with a branded template, signup forms and QR codes, and a regular issue written for you if you want one. It is how we stay in front of our own customers.

Proposals and business plans

Vendor quote reviews, board summaries and sales proposals: clear numbers, a clear recommendation and a plan a decision-maker can sign off on.

Sales and print materials

Rate sheets, flyers, signup cards with QR codes, and promo graphics sized for every platform, all matched to your brand.

Tools that keep people on the page

Calculators, quote estimators and preview tools built into your site, like our own field calculators. A visitor who uses one stays longer and calls more often.

Maps

Service-area maps for your website that show where you work without giving away what you want kept private, and delivery maps with drop pins and QR codes for your crews.

Growth planning

Which towns and counties to go after next, in what order, and what has to be true before each move. Each step pays for itself before the next one starts.

How it works

  1. We build a draft

Questions we get

Do I have to be in agriculture?

No. Farming is our background, not a requirement. If you serve local customers, the work is the same.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can rebuild it, move it, or tune the one you have so Google finds it. We moved our own site off a website builder in 2026, so we know what that takes.

Can I make changes myself?

You can, but most people would rather text us. Small updates are included in every monthly plan: send what you want changed and we take care of it.
